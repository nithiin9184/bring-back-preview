## 1. What This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy explains how N Connect may collect, use, store, protect, disclose, and delete information when you use the service.

## 2. Information You Provide

Depending on the features you use, N Connect may process:

- Phone number used for account authentication and OTP delivery.
- Name, username, bio, and location information you choose to provide.
- Profile photo and adjusted/cropped profile photo.
- Permanent 7-digit N Connect Unique ID.
- QR representation of the Unique ID.
- Follow, Contact, Trust, block, report, and request relationships.
- Messages and images you send through chats.
- Status updates and their audience/view information.
- Call-related information such as call state/history and technical connection information where required for calling.
- Backup settings, backup metadata, and backup content where backup is enabled.
- Connect Credit purchases, Verification Badge status, and entitlement information.
- Feedback, support requests, and reports.

## 3. Authentication Information

N Connect uses phone-based authentication and one-time verification codes.

Your phone number is used for authentication and service operations. It is not intended to be publicly displayed as your N Connect identity.

OTP delivery may use a third-party delivery/integration service. Only the minimum information required for delivery and authentication should be shared with that provider.

## 4. Profile and Social Information

Information such as your name, username, profile photo, bio, selected location, follow relationships, and other profile information may be visible according to your settings and the product's functionality.

Your permanent 7-digit Unique ID may be displayed in your own profile and represented by your QR code. Your phone number remains private unless disclosure is required by law or you separately choose a supported disclosure.

## 5. Messages, Images and Status

N Connect processes communications so that messages, images, and Status updates can be delivered and displayed to the intended audience.

Disappearing messages and Status updates may be automatically removed according to their configured expiry rules.

Recipients may still retain information before expiry through screenshots, photographs, recordings, downloads, or other means outside N Connect's control.

## 6. Contacts, Search, Trust and Blocking

N Connect may process relationship and discovery information to provide Search, Connect, Contacts, Follow, Trust, blocking, and reporting features.

Blocking may cause accounts to be excluded from discovery, Contact/QR resolution, Status visibility, suggestions, messaging, and other interactions.

## 7. Backup and Restore

If you enable Chat Backup, N Connect may process backup content and backup metadata needed to create, maintain, delete, and restore backups.

The product may support:
- manual backup;
- automatic backup;
- Daily, Weekly, or Monthly schedules;
- optional media backup where available;
- backup deletion;
- restore to a supported installation.

Where backup encryption is used, the applicable encryption design and key-management model determine who can access the backup. If N Connect cannot recover a user-controlled backup key, the user may permanently lose access to an encrypted backup.

Backup data must not be treated as a way to resurrect data that has already been legitimately deleted or expired under the service's retention rules.

Depending on the production implementation, N Connect may process technical information such as:

- app version;
- operating system and device characteristics;
- IP address and network information;
- crash/error information;
- security and abuse-prevention signals;
- notification/device-token information;
- technical information required for voice/video calling.

Only information actually collected in production should be disclosed in the final Play Console Data Safety form.

## 9. Camera, Microphone and Media

Camera access may be used for profile photos, Status creation, or other user-initiated media features.

Microphone access may be used for voice calls, video calls, or other explicitly user-initiated communication features.

Permissions should be requested only when needed for the relevant feature and according to Android and Google Play requirements.

## 10. How We Use Information

N Connect may use information to:

- create and authenticate accounts;
- deliver messages, Status, notifications, and calls;
- operate Search, Connect, Contacts, Follow, and Trust;
- provide backup and restore;
- process purchases and entitlements;
- prevent abuse, fraud, spam, and unauthorized access;
- respond to reports and support requests;
- maintain, secure, debug, and improve the service;
- comply with legal obligations.

## 11. Sharing and Third Parties

N Connect may use service providers for infrastructure and operations, such as authentication/OTP delivery, cloud hosting, storage, realtime services, payments, notifications, calling infrastructure, analytics, security, and advertising where applicable.

Providers should receive only the information reasonably necessary for their service.

N Connect should not claim that a provider receives data unless that provider is actually used in production.

## 12. Advertising

If advertisements are enabled, N Connect may use an advertising provider that processes certain device or advertising information according to its own policies and the permissions/disclosures applicable to the app.

The final Privacy Policy and Play Console Data Safety form must identify the actual advertising SDKs used in production.

## 13. Payments

Payment information may be processed by the applicable app store or payment provider. N Connect should not store full payment-card credentials unless the production architecture explicitly requires and lawfully supports it.

## 14. Data Security

N Connect uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information.

Security measures may include authentication controls, authorization, encryption in transit, protected storage, access controls, rate limiting, abuse prevention, audit logging, and secure deletion.

No system can guarantee absolute security.

## 15. Data Retention

N Connect retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, the service's operation, security, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and legitimate fraud-prevention needs.

Retention periods may differ by data type.

Expired Status and disappearing content should not be retained beyond their configured lifecycle unless a lawful technical, security, or legal reason requires temporary retention.

## 16. Account Deletion

N Connect provides an in-app account deletion path.

Google Play requires apps that allow account creation to provide a readily discoverable in-app deletion mechanism and an external web resource for deletion requests. Account freezing or deactivation alone does not satisfy the deletion requirement. citeturn0search0turn0search3

When an account is deleted, N Connect should delete associated user data according to the deletion workflow, except information that must or may lawfully be retained for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or other disclosed reasons.

The final production release must also provide the required external account-deletion web resource.

## 17. Data Export

Where N Connect provides a Download Your Data feature, the export may contain information associated with the user's account and supported data categories.

Export files should be protected and should have a defined retention/expiry period.
