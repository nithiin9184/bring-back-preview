## 1. Purpose

N Connect is designed for professional social discovery and private communication. Users are expected to communicate respectfully and use the service lawfully.

These standards apply to profiles, usernames, bios, Status updates, messages, images, calls, reports, and other user-generated material or interactions supported by N Connect.

## 2. Prohibited Conduct

Do not use N Connect to:

- threaten, harass, stalk, intimidate, or repeatedly abuse another person;
- impersonate another person or organization;
- operate scams, phishing schemes, fraud, or deceptive financial activity;
- distribute malware or attempt unauthorized access;
- distribute illegal content or facilitate illegal activity;
- share another person's private or sensitive information without authorization;
- send spam or unwanted bulk communications;
- evade blocks, safety restrictions, or enforcement actions;
- exploit vulnerabilities or abuse the service;
- use the service to facilitate exploitation or abuse of children.

## 3. Child Safety

N Connect has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation.

The creation, upload, request, distribution, promotion, or facilitation of child sexual abuse material or exploitation is prohibited.

Reports involving suspected child exploitation must be handled through the service's safety process and applicable law. N Connect will take appropriate action when it obtains actual knowledge of prohibited material or conduct.

Google Play's current Child Safety Standards require Social and Dating apps to publish standards, provide an in-app feedback mechanism, address CSAM, comply with child-safety laws, and provide a child-safety point of contact. citeturn0search9turn0search11

## 4. Sexual Content and Exploitation

Do not use N Connect to exploit, coerce, traffic, or abuse another person.

Sexual content involving minors is strictly prohibited.

The final product's age restrictions and handling of adult content must follow the applicable Google Play policy and the laws of the markets where N Connect is offered.

## 5. Harassment and Bullying

Do not repeatedly contact someone after they have clearly indicated they do not want contact.

Use Block, Report, Contact Requests, Message Requests, Trust controls, and privacy settings to manage unwanted interactions.

## 6. Privacy and Doxxing

Do not publish or send private information about another person without an appropriate basis or permission.

Examples include private phone numbers, passwords, authentication codes, financial credentials, precise private addresses, or confidential documents.

N Connect's reporting system may treat privacy violations as a safety issue.

## 7. Scams, Spam and Fraud

Do not use N Connect for:

- fake investment or payment schemes;
- impersonation scams;
- credential theft;
- OTP requests;
- malicious links;
- fraudulent sales;
- coordinated spam;
- deceptive requests for money or sensitive information.

Never share your N Connect OTP or backup key with another person.

## 8. Threats and Illegal Activity

Do not use N Connect to threaten violence, coordinate serious criminal activity, or distribute material whose possession or distribution is unlawful.

Where legally required or reasonably necessary for immediate safety, N Connect may cooperate with lawful requests from authorities.

## 9. User-Generated Content

Users remain responsible for content they create or share.

N Connect may remove, restrict, hide, or disable content or accounts when required by law, these standards, safety considerations, or legitimate enforcement processes.

## 10. Reporting

Users should report content or behavior that violates these standards using the in-app Report function where available.

A report should contain enough information for N Connect to identify the relevant account, message, content, or behavior.

Reports may be reviewed using automated or human-assisted processes where appropriate.

## 11. Blocking

Blocking is a user safety control.

A blocked person may be prevented from messaging, calling, discovering, resolving through Contact/QR flows, seeing Status updates, or interacting with the blocker as determined by the product.

Users should not attempt to circumvent another person's block through alternate accounts or other abusive means.

## 12. Enforcement

Depending on severity and circumstances, enforcement may include:

- warning;
- content removal or restriction;
- messaging or feature restrictions;
- temporary suspension;
- permanent account termination;
- blocking related accounts or abuse patterns;
- referral to appropriate authorities where legally required or appropriate.

N Connect may act without prior notice where immediate action is necessary to protect users, preserve evidence, prevent abuse, or comply with law.

## 13. Appeals

Where an appeal process is offered, users may request review of an enforcement decision through the available support channel.

An appeal should explain why the decision may have been incorrect and provide relevant information.
